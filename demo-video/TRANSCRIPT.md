# SpeakUp: demo walkthrough narration (1:53)

A plain walkthrough voiceover for `speakup_demo.mp4`. Each section says what is on screen
and how that part of the app works. The timestamps match the video. Word counts are rough,
for a normal speaking pace (about 150 words a minute). Lines in *italics* describe the
screen and are not read aloud.

---

**0:00 – 0:03** · *Title card* (about 8 words)

> This is SpeakUp, a debate practice app built for HackNC.

**0:03 – 0:13** · *The 3D lobby, Solo Speak, choosing a level and a topic, the question appears* (about 25 words)

> You start in a 3D lobby. In Solo Speak, you pick a difficulty level and a topic category. Then Gemini writes a yes-or-no question for you to argue.

**0:13 – 0:16** · *Countdown from the podium* (about 8 words)

> Your character walks to the podium, and a five-second countdown starts.

**0:16 – 0:23** · *The first seconds of the speech, at normal speed. Stay silent.*

**0:23 – 0:34** · *The rest of the speech at 5x* (about 25 words)

> You have up to sixty seconds to argue your side out loud. The bar along the bottom shows your time, going from white to red as it runs out.

**0:34 – 0:36** · *Applause while the speech is graded (sped up)* (about 5 words)

> The crowd applauds while it grades.

**0:36 – 0:47** · *"Under the hood" card* (about 28 words)

> ElevenLabs Scribe turns the recording into a transcript, with the timing of every word, including the ums. Gemini then maps out the argument, and our own code checks it and calculates the scores.

**0:47 – 1:10** · *Scrolling down the speech card* (about 58 words)

> This is the results card. At the top is your overall grade, split into logic, emotion, and delivery. Below that are the fixes that would raise your score the most, and a radar chart of your strengths and weaknesses. Next is your transcript, with fillers, long pauses, and flat stretches marked. Then each claim you made is listed with the evidence you used. At the bottom are all twenty-five metrics. Each one opens to a short coaching tip from Gemini.

**1:10 – 1:14** · *The Daily Prompt at the podium* (about 10 words)

> The Daily Prompt is one question that everyone answers each day.

**1:14 – 1:18** · *The Daily Prompt answer. Stay silent.*

**1:18 – 1:29** · *The Daily Prompt results card, then sharing to the community board* (about 28 words)

> It's graded the same way, and compared with your best attempt so far. You can share your speech to the community board. Shared speeches are reviewed, and deleted when the day's round ends.

**1:29 – 1:39** · *Another account plays the speech and likes it* (about 25 words)

> Here, another player opens the board and plays the speech. Players can react with love, like, fire, or amazing. This one picks like, and the count updates.

**1:39 – 1:46** · *Community favorites, then the three difficulty leaderboards* (about 18 words)

> Community favorites ranks speeches by their reactions. Below it are three leaderboards, one for each difficulty level.

**1:46 – 1:53** · *Closing card* (about 15 words)

> That's SpeakUp. You can try it at speak-up dot fly dot dev.

## Notes for recording

- **Silent parts:** stay quiet during 0:16 – 0:23 and 1:14 – 1:18. Those are your own speeches at normal speed.
- **Where to talk over the footage:** the narration at 0:23 – 0:34 goes over the 5x speech. The fast audio is lowered under your voice when the voiceover is mixed in.
- **Recording it:** one take is easiest. Pause a beat between sections and I'll place each one at its timestamp, the same as the last voiceover.
